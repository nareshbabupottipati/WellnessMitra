# FITNESS EXERCISES KNOWLEDGE BASE
# WellnessMitra RAG Knowledge Document

## STRENGTH TRAINING EXERCISES

### Push-Up
Target Muscles: Chest (pectoralis major), Triceps, Front Deltoids, Core
How to perform: Start in a high plank position with hands shoulder-width apart. Lower your chest to the ground keeping elbows at 45 degrees. Push back up to starting position.
Sets/Reps: Beginner 3x8-10 | Intermediate 3x15-20 | Advanced 4x25+
Form Tips: Keep core tight, don't let hips sag, breathe out on the way up.
Modifications: Knee push-ups for beginners, incline push-ups on a table.

### Squat
Target Muscles: Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Glutes, Core
How to perform: Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly turned out. Lower hips as if sitting in a chair, keeping chest up and knees tracking over toes. Return to standing.
Sets/Reps: 3x10-15 | Advanced 4x20 with weight
Form Tips: Keep heels on the ground, don't let knees cave inward, depth to parallel or below.
Modifications: Chair squats for beginners, goblet squats with dumbbell.

### Plank
Target Muscles: Core (transverse abdominis, rectus abdominis), Shoulders, Back
How to perform: Hold a push-up position on forearms. Keep body in a straight line from head to heels.
Duration: Beginner 20-30 seconds | Intermediate 45-60 seconds | Advanced 90+ seconds
Form Tips: Don't let hips rise or sag, breathe normally, engage glutes.

### Deadlift
Target Muscles: Hamstrings, Glutes, Lower Back, Traps, Core
How to perform: Stand with feet hip-width apart, bar over mid-foot. Hinge at hips, grip bar outside legs. Lift by pushing floor away, keeping back straight.
Sets/Reps: 3x5-8 (focus on form before increasing weight)
Form Tips: Neutral spine throughout, bar stays close to body, drive hips forward at top.

### Pull-Up / Chin-Up
Target Muscles: Latissimus Dorsi, Biceps, Rear Deltoids
How to perform: Hang from bar with overhand grip (pull-up) or underhand grip (chin-up). Pull body up until chin clears bar, lower with control.
Sets/Reps: 3x5-12 depending on strength level
Modifications: Assisted machine, resistance band support, or negative pull-ups.

### Lunges
Target Muscles: Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Glutes, Balance
How to perform: Step forward with one foot, lower back knee toward floor. Push off front foot to return. Alternate legs.
Sets/Reps: 3x10-12 each leg
Form Tips: Keep front knee behind toes, torso upright, controlled movement.

## CARDIO EXERCISES

### High Knees
Intensity: High | Calories: ~10/minute
Technique: Run in place bringing knees to hip height alternately. Pump arms for full body engagement.
Duration: 30-60 seconds bursts | Rest 30 seconds | Repeat 3-5 times

### Jumping Jacks
Intensity: Moderate | Calories: ~8/minute
Technique: Jump feet out while raising arms overhead, return to start.
Good for: Warm-up, active recovery, cardiovascular conditioning

### Burpees
Intensity: Very High | Calories: ~12-15/minute
Technique: Stand, drop to plank, do push-up, jump feet to hands, jump up with arms overhead.
Sets: 3-5 rounds of 10 reps with 60 seconds rest

## FLEXIBILITY & MOBILITY

### Hip Flexor Stretch
Hold: 30-60 seconds each side
Position: Low lunge position, back knee on ground, push hips forward gently.
When: Post-workout or morning routine to counter sitting.

### Hamstring Stretch
Hold: 30-45 seconds
Position: Seated or standing, reach toward toes keeping back straight.
Benefit: Reduces lower back pain, improves posture.

### Shoulder Mobility
Exercise: Arm circles (forward/backward 30 seconds each), cross-body shoulder stretch, doorway chest stretch.
