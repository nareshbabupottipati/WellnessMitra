# NUTRITION & DIET GUIDELINES
# WellnessMitra RAG Knowledge Document

## MACRONUTRIENTS

### Protein
Role: Muscle repair and growth, immune function, enzyme production
Sources: Chicken, fish, eggs, lentils (dal), chickpeas, paneer, tofu, Greek yogurt
Daily Target: 0.8-1g per kg body weight (general) | 1.6-2.2g/kg (muscle building)
Timing: Consume within 30-60 minutes post-workout for optimal muscle recovery

### Carbohydrates
Role: Primary energy source, brain fuel, glycogen stores
Complex Carbs (preferred): Brown rice, oats, sweet potato, whole wheat roti, quinoa
Simple Carbs (limit): White rice, white bread, sugar, processed foods
Daily Target: 45-65% of total calories | Higher for endurance athletes

### Fats
Role: Hormone production, vitamin absorption, joint health
Healthy Fats: Avocado, nuts, seeds, olive oil, coconut oil, fatty fish
Saturated Fats (moderate): Ghee, coconut, dairy
Avoid: Trans fats in processed/fried foods
Daily Target: 25-35% of total calories

## MEAL TIMING

Pre-Workout (1-2 hours before):
- Light carbs + moderate protein
- Example: Banana with peanut butter, oats with milk, fruit + yogurt

Post-Workout (30-60 minutes after):
- Protein + fast carbs to replenish glycogen
- Example: Protein shake with banana, chicken rice bowl, dal rice

## WEIGHT LOSS NUTRITION

Calorie Deficit: 300-500 calories below TDEE
Protein Priority: High protein (1.8-2.2g/kg) to preserve muscle
Fiber: 25-35g daily to promote satiety — vegetables, fruits, legumes
Avoid: Liquid calories, ultra-processed foods, excessive refined sugars
Hydration: 2.5-3.5 liters of water daily

Sample Indian Weight Loss Day:
- Breakfast: Moong dal chilla (2) + green chutney + black coffee
- Snack: Apple + 10 almonds
- Lunch: Brown rice (1 cup) + rajma (1 cup) + cucumber salad
- Snack: Greek yogurt + berries
- Dinner: Grilled paneer tikka (150g) + sautéed vegetables + 1 roti

## MUSCLE GAIN NUTRITION

Calorie Surplus: 200-350 calories above TDEE
Protein: 1.8-2.2g per kg body weight
Carbohydrates: Higher intake to fuel workouts and recovery
Meal Frequency: 4-6 smaller meals for sustained amino acid delivery

Sample Indian Muscle Building Day:
- Breakfast: 4 egg omelet + 2 whole wheat toast + banana + milk
- Snack: Peanut butter sandwich + protein shake
- Lunch: Chicken (200g) + rice (1.5 cups) + dal + vegetables
- Snack: Curd (200g) + mixed nuts + fruit
- Post-Workout: Whey protein shake + banana
- Dinner: Fish curry (200g) + quinoa (1 cup) + green salad

## VEGETARIAN PROTEIN SOURCES

High Protein Options:
- Paneer: 18g protein per 100g
- Lentils (dal): 9g protein per 100g cooked
- Chickpeas: 8.9g protein per 100g cooked
- Tofu: 8g protein per 100g
- Greek yogurt: 10g protein per 100g
- Quinoa: 4.4g protein per 100g cooked (complete protein)
- Edamame: 11g protein per 100g

## HYDRATION GUIDELINES

Daily Water Intake: Body weight (kg) × 0.033 = liters needed
Additional: Add 500ml per hour of exercise
Signs of dehydration: Dark urine, fatigue, headaches, reduced performance
Best practice: Start day with 500ml water, drink consistently throughout day

## SUPPLEMENTS (Optional)

Evidence-based supplements:
- Whey Protein: Convenient post-workout protein source
- Creatine Monohydrate: 5g daily — proven for strength and muscle
- Vitamin D3: 1000-2000 IU daily (especially for indoor workers)
- Omega-3 Fish Oil: 1-3g EPA+DHA daily for inflammation and heart health
- Magnesium: 200-400mg before bed for sleep and muscle recovery

Note: Food first — supplements are just that, supplemental.
