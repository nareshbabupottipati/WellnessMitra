# WELLNESS & MENTAL HEALTH GUIDELINES
# WellnessMitra RAG Knowledge Document

## SLEEP OPTIMIZATION

Recommended Sleep: 7-9 hours for adults
Sleep and Fitness: Growth hormone released primarily during deep sleep — critical for muscle repair
Sleep Debt: Reduces testosterone, increases cortisol, impairs glucose metabolism

Sleep Hygiene Tips:
- Consistent schedule: Same bed and wake time even on weekends
- Dark, cool room (18-20°C is optimal for sleep)
- No screens 60 minutes before bed (blue light suppresses melatonin)
- Avoid caffeine after 2 PM
- Pre-sleep routine: Reading, light stretching, journaling

Sleep-Enhancing Foods:
- Tryptophan-rich: Milk, bananas, turkey, nuts — consumed 2 hours before bed
- Magnesium: Leafy greens, pumpkin seeds, dark chocolate — promotes relaxation

## STRESS MANAGEMENT

Impact on Fitness: Chronic stress raises cortisol → fat storage, muscle breakdown, poor recovery

Box Breathing Technique (4-4-4-4):
1. Inhale through nose for 4 counts
2. Hold breath for 4 counts
3. Exhale through mouth for 4 counts
4. Hold empty for 4 counts
Repeat 4-6 cycles. Use before workouts, meetings, or when feeling overwhelmed.

Progressive Muscle Relaxation:
- Tense each muscle group for 5 seconds, then release for 30 seconds
- Start from toes, move upward to face
- Duration: 15-20 minutes before bed

Mindfulness Practice:
- Daily 10-minute meditation reduces cortisol by up to 20%
- Apps: Headspace, Calm, Insight Timer
- Simple technique: Focus on breath, notice thoughts without judgment, return to breath

## RECOVERY & REST

Active Recovery Days:
- Light walking (20-30 min), yoga, swimming, cycling at low intensity
- Promotes blood flow without adding training stress
- Essential for preventing overtraining syndrome

Signs of Overtraining:
- Persistent fatigue lasting more than 2 days
- Decreased performance despite continued training
- Mood disturbances: irritability, depression, lack of motivation
- Frequent illness (suppressed immune system)
- Sleep disturbances

Recovery Tools:
- Foam Rolling: Self-myofascial release, 60 seconds per muscle group
- Cold Therapy: Cold shower or ice bath post intense workout reduces inflammation
- Contrast Therapy: Alternating hot-cold exposure
- Epsom Salt Bath: Magnesium absorption, muscle relaxation

## WORKOUT CONSISTENCY TIPS

Habit Formation:
- It takes ~66 days to form a habit (not 21 as commonly believed)
- "Never miss twice" rule — one missed workout is fine, two starts a pattern
- Schedule workouts like appointments — block calendar time

Motivation vs Discipline:
- Motivation is unreliable — build systems and discipline instead
- Start with the minimum viable workout on low-motivation days (even 10 minutes counts)
- Track progress visually — seeing improvements is self-reinforcing

Social Accountability:
- Working out with a partner increases adherence by 95%
- Sharing goals publicly creates social commitment
- Join fitness communities (online or local)

## INJURY PREVENTION

Warm-Up Importance:
- 5-10 minutes of dynamic stretching before workouts
- Raises muscle temperature, improves joint mobility, activates neural pathways
- Dynamic warm-up: Leg swings, arm circles, hip circles, light jogging

Common Injuries and Prevention:
- Lower Back Pain: Strengthen core, maintain neutral spine, avoid rounding back
- Knee Pain: Strengthen VMO (inner quad), ensure proper squat/lunge form
- Shoulder Impingement: Balance pushing/pulling exercises, strengthen rotator cuff
- Shin Splints: Gradual mileage increase (10% rule), proper footwear

RICE Protocol for Minor Injuries:
- Rest: Stop activity causing pain
- Ice: 20 minutes every 2-3 hours in first 48 hours
- Compression: Elastic bandage to reduce swelling
- Elevation: Raise injured area above heart level
